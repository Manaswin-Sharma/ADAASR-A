STATE 1----> LAUNCHING PAD STABLE
    THE GYROSCOPE AND THE BAROMETER DETECTS THAT IT IS ON THE GROUND AND NO FORCES ARE CURRENTLY ACTING ON THE ROCKET THUS LOCKING THE SERVOS 
    AT A STRCIT 90 ANGLE TO OREVENT ANY ERROR DURING A LAUNCHING 

STATE 2 ---> LAUNCHED
    THE GYROSCOPE AND BAROMETER AUTOMATICALLY DETECTS THAT THE ROCKET HAS ENGAGED ITS FLIGHT OF IGNITION AND IMMIDIETLY STARTS TO STUDY ROLL PITCH AND YAW
    WHILE ORDERING THE SERVOS USING PID LOOPS TO ANGLE THE GIMBALS AND FIX THE PITCH AND YAW
    SEPRATELY SINCE THE TVC GIMBAL WILL AUTOMATICALLY BE USELESS AFTER THE BURNOFF PHASE ADDITIONLLY AIRELOIN FLAPS HAVE BEEN ADDED TO THE ROCKETS 
    TO FIX IT PITCH AND YAW AND SINCE GIMBAL SYSTEM HAS NO CONTROLL OVER ROLL THE FLAPS ALSO FIX AND ROLL TO THE ROCKET

STATE 3 --->LAUNCHED AFTER THE BURNOFF PHASE 
    NOW SINCE THE GIMBAL IS USELESS THE THE WHOLE AUTHORITY WILL BE HANDED TO THE FLAPS THE FLAPS WILL HAVE AUTHORITY EVEN BEFORE THIS PHASE BECUASE THEIR THE ONLY ONES
    WHO CAN FIS THE ROLL

STATE 4 ---> APOOGEE 
    THE ACCELEROMETER AND THE BAROMETER TOGETHER CALCULATE AND WHEN THE APOOGEEE IS REACHED THE AURDINO SENDS A SHARP 240 DEGREE TURN TO THE PARACHUTE HACHET SERVO TO 
    OPEN THE DOOR AND SPRINGS FLING THE PARACHUTE OUTSIDE TO PROVIDE THE SMART RECOVERY FUNCTION
